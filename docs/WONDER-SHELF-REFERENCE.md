# Wonder Shelf — reference observations

Reviewed 6 October 2026 in the browser. Wonder Shelf is a quality reference, not a design to reproduce. No reference artwork, source code, titles, characters, or compositions were imported.

## Scope and correction

The first pass covered the [homepage](https://wonder-shelf.web.app/), [catalogue](https://wonder-shelf.web.app/games/) and [Ricochet Orchard guide](https://wonder-shelf.web.app/games/ricochet-orchard/). That established useful navigation patterns but did not establish how the games actually looked or played. A second pass opened the real game hosts and entered gameplay in Ricochet Orchard and Tandem Tug. Parcel Pond's title screen was also inspected. These observations are not audience, retention, popularity, or full game-completion evidence.

## Actual game screens

- [Ricochet Orchard](https://ricochet-orchard-play.web.app/): a viewport-filling arena, compact score/time/sound/pause HUD, paddles integrated into a themed field, and short control instructions. Starting play produces a visible countdown; a missed return produces a large, temporary result cue and updated score. Its title screen and buttons also belong to the game's visual world.
- [Tandem Tug](https://tandem-tug-play.web.app/): the harbor is the screen, with textured water, detailed moving boats, gates and destination markings. Mission, hull, time and pause controls occupy a compact top strip. Starting a voyage shows a countdown; the CPU tug visibly navigates and docks. Independent boat input and complete voyage progression were not exhaustively tested.
- [Parcel Pond](https://parcel-pond-play.web.app/): the title screen already presents a recognizable canal-routing board, gates, destinations and parcels. Its play action includes the current shift and its progress is readable. This inspection did not enter its shift gameplay.

## Transferable principles

1. Give the playable arena most of the viewport. Avoid surrounding a small canvas with a large website layout.
2. Use distinct material, typography and control styling for each game.
3. Make gameplay objects readable and finished: depth, lighting and surface details should preserve collision boundaries.
4. Show cause and effect: ball trails, impacts, shots, damage and score changes should be understandable while playing.
5. Keep start, pause and results inside the same game world, with short instructions and a clear next action.

Our original directions are an electric Breakpoint arena, an Asteroid Run flight console, and a Lane Rush motorsport cockpit. These games keep their own mechanics, names, palettes and compositions. The reference's orchard, harbor, canal, characters and branded buttons are not reproduced.

## Homepage application

The root now lists eleven standalone games. Every preview is a screenshot of the local game's real renderer; the hero also offers one opt-in live Breakpoint demonstration. The three new arcade games lead the collection. Game facts, player modes and expandable keyboard/touch instructions reduce uncertainty before launching. A direct Word Weave daily link and filtered random selection remain available. On phones, the featured game screen appears before the longer introductory copy.

The earlier decorative cover illustrations are no longer used. The homepage retains its own editorial type and cream, green and rust palette. No audience-growth claims are inferred from visual observations.
