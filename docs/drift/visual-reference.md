# Deadshot visual reference

Reference: [Deadshot.io](https://deadshot.io/), inspected live on 1 October 2026: lobby, class selector, assault-rifle gameplay in a stone courtyard, and a nearby player model. Also compared the two screenshots supplied for this task.

The target is restrained, low-poly realism. Recognizable construction, human silhouettes and coherent materials contribute more than additional small geometric details.

| Area | Observed in the reference | Direction for Drift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Characters | Fitted clothing, continuous sleeves, restrained tactical equipment, readable hair and face, natural shoulder transitions. The supplied tuxedo character and live tactical character share a human silhouette. | Shape a clothed body first; use equipment and small accents to distinguish classes. Reduce repeated bright armor plates and disconnected joints. |
| Weapons | Recognizable stock, receiver, barrel and grip proportions; open rounded sight; dark worn surfaces; substantial first-person foreground presence. | Coherent weapon silhouettes, curved handguards, restrained edge wear, clear hand contact and an unobstructed sight. |
| Architecture | Stone courses, mortar joints, arches, coping, ironwork and paving establish familiar scale. The supplied interior adds wooden panels and patterned carpet. | Add material-specific construction detail appropriate to each original arena: formwork, paving, dressed stone and sheet-metal seams. |
| Surroundings | Clouded daylight, vegetation, recognizably different outdoor and recessed areas. | Natural sky, consistent lighting direction, grounded shading and distinct surface finishes. |
| Presentation | Lobby character remains sharp against a blurred background; gameplay preserves clear targets and landmarks. | Keep portraits readable and surface detail restrained at distance. |

The reference does not establish its polygon counts, asset licenses, texture resolutions, or whether its shading is baked or physically based. Its death-screen overlay is not reliable evidence of normal gameplay brightness. No reference models, textures or map layouts are copied into Drift.
