## Design Context

### Users
Desktop browser players who want a lightweight multiplayer shooter, Apex-inspired movement, and the quick accessibility of Deadshot.io. Keyboard and mouse only.

### Brand Personality
Modern, lightweight, interactive. Movement and social play are the core experience. DRIFT is a provisional prototype name.

### Aesthetic Direction
Implementation direction: a sunlit architectural arena, warm concrete, blue sky, vermilion traversal markings, dark ink interface, ivory typography. Simple geometry, no downloaded texture packs, limited real-time effects. The user has not selected specific brand colors.

### Design Principles
- Instant comprehension: one clear play action, readable controls and combat feedback.
- Spend geometry and effects on spatial clarity and movement feel.
- Small download and bounded rendering costs throughout.
- Desktop keyboard controls, visible focus, adjustable sensitivity, and reduced camera motion.
- Keep interface details quiet during play.
